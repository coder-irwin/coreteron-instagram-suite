Handling Captchas and Phone Verification

Instagram occasionally shows a visual image check or phone verification. The system includes Human-in-the-Loop protection:

  • The dashboard alerts you with a notification: "Human Verification In Browser".
  • Look at the open browser window, solve the visual check or enter your mobile number.
  • Click "I Solved It - Resume Automation" on the dashboard, or wait a few seconds. The bot will automatically detect completion and resume.
